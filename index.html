Shivam's Math Path

Five focused sessions designed to identify strengths, close gaps, and build confidence with expressions and equations.

Your Goal

Build the algebra foundation that makes the next unit easier

We will begin with a low-pressure skills check. The goal is not to earn a grade. It is to discover exactly what Shivam already knows and where a little targeted practice will create the biggest improvement.

Bring: pencil, scratch paper, and a willingness to explain your thinking. A calculator is not needed for the intake assessment.

Equations

Session 1: Algebra Readiness Assessment

Work one problem at a time. Show scratch work and explain selected answers aloud. Enter only the final answer in each box.

Tutor pacing: 5-minute welcome, 40–45 minutes assessment and discussion, 5-minute stretch/reset, 5–10 minute reflection and preview.
